Surface setup
  1. Mount the camera so it sees the whole projected area. Keep the camera and projector fixed.
  2. Open the projector window on the second display, make it fullscreen, and enable corner targets.
  3. In the depth image below, click those targets in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.
  4. Save mapping. Clear the surface, then capture its depth.
