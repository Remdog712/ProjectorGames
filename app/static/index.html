Surface setup
  1. Mount the camera so it sees the whole projected area. Keep the camera and projector fixed.
  2. Open the projector window on the second display, make it fullscreen, and enable corner targets.
  3. In the color image below, click those targets in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.
  4. Align color and depth using the guide below. Save mapping, clear the surface, then capture its depth.

The Astra Pro uses separate sensors. Place a hand close to the wall at four positions spread around the play area in clockwise order. At each position, freeze the images, click the same fingertip in color and depth, then resume before moving to the next position. Use the same visible physical point, not a projected mark. This approximates alignment at the wall plane.
