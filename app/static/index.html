BODY PLAY

Your movement.
Your playground.

Set up from this computer. Timed captures give you time to stand in view and return to select points.

1 · Connect cameras

The app searches the Orbbec installation folder on Windows. Paste a different folder if needed; settings are remembered.

If you see the PC webcam, try 1 or 2. Camera numbers can differ by backend. Close Windows Camera first.

Move one hand: it should appear on the same side in both previews. Flip one view if needed, then reselect alignment and distance points. This changes tracking coordinates as well as the preview.

2 · Click the surface distance

Click below, then click one solid wall/floor spot in the depth image. Its measured distance becomes the surface / lower boundary. No empty-scene capture or touching the wall is needed.

Surface not selected.

3 · Mark the projected area

Extend your Windows displays. Move the game window onto the projector and double-click for fullscreen. From this PC, click the four outer rectangle corners in the color image: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. No touching the wall.

0 / 4 corners

4 · Capture your body pose

Click capture, stand near the wall facing the camera, spread your arms and feet like a star, and hold still until the countdown finishes. Return to the PC: both images stay frozen on the server.

Select the same four points in color and depth: your right hand, your left hand, your left foot, your right foot. Always choose the SAME physical limb in both images, even if its image position is mirrored. Use visible points on your body; you do not need to touch a corner.

Pairing approximates color/depth alignment near the surface. Keep the pose close to the wall and check ring positions afterward. You can redo pairing without resetting the projected corners.

5 · Click the closest body distance

Stand at the closest position you want to allow. Click capture, move into position, and hold still until the countdown finishes. Come back to the PC and click your chest ONCE in the frozen depth image.

Select a surface point, then a chest point.

Everything between the chest depth and the surface depth can interact inside the projected play area. Anything closer to the camera than your chest, or behind the selected surface, is ignored. These are camera depth distances, not anatomical height or a fitted wall plane.

By default, ignore the last 1 cm next to the surface so the wall itself does not trigger rings. Set 0 to include the exact selected surface distance. Green in Active regions shows the saved playable band.

Play

Your body controls the game. The mouse only operates setup controls. Whole-body mode samples silhouette edges and centers; it does not label anatomical joints.
