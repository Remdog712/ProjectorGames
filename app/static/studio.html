FROM CONNECTION TO PLAY

A simpler setup.

Map the projector automatically, measure the surface, then align the body silhouette. No clicking eight hands and feet.

Camera connection

1

Clear the wall or floor

Open the projector window, move it onto the projector, and double-click for fullscreen. Keep the empty, flat surface in both camera views.

Four coded squares will be detected automatically.

2

One pose. Automatic alignment.

Stand near the surface with arms and feet apart; hold one hand higher than the other so left/right can be distinguished. After the countdown, return to the PC and click Auto align. The images remain frozen.

Verify your silhouette against the Registered overlay.

3

Set the playable distance

The measured surface compensates for a tilted wall. Set the interaction distance in Noise & tracking, or measure it from your chest.

Calibration quality & recovery

Not calibrated.

Automatic alignment matches RGB/depth silhouettes at one plane; it is not factory 3D registration. Hold still, keep the background unchanged, avoid shadows, and retry if the overlay is offset. Existing calibration is kept when detection fails. Re-run setup after moving the devices or changing resolution.

LIVE FEEDBACK

Steadier tracking.

Left smooths jitter; right follows faster. Two-frame stability rejects flashes at the cost of one camera frame.

Person tracking & skeletons

Tracked body regions have persistent IDs and smoothed movement. They can merge when people touch. They are not skeletal identities.

Optional 17-joint pose inference can use an exported YOLOv8-pose ONNX model. Windows GPU inference requires ONNX Runtime DirectML; CPU fallback is available. Weights are not included.
